# Demo storyboard and narration

Generated by `render_media.py storyboard` from the scene list in `render_video.py`. Times are those of the rendered video. The narration is a script for a future voice-over: **no voice-over exists**; the videos are silent with burned-in captions.

## Recording a voice-over (not done)

1. Record each scene's narration in a quiet room, in your own voice or with a text-to-speech service whose terms allow publishing the audio. Do not imitate or clone anyone's voice without their written permission.
2. Keep each recording within its scene's time span below (the captions use the same spans); speak the English and the Arabic version separately.
3. Combine the clips into one track per language and add it to the matching video with a full FFmpeg build (Playwright's build has no audio encoder), for example `ffmpeg -i demo-en.webm -i narration-en.ogg -c:v copy -c:a libopus -shortest demo-en-voice.webm`.
4. Play the result to the end, check that speech and captions agree, and label the voice ("narration: <name>" or "synthetic voice: <service>") in docs/demo.md.
